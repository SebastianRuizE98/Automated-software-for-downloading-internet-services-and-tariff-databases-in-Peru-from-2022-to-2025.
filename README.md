# Automated-software-for-downloading-internet-services-and-tariff-databases-in-Peru-from-2022-to-2025.
This project involves the development of automated software designed to download and retrieve data from the internet services and tariffs database in Peru, covering the period from 2022 to 2025. The purpose of this tool is to streamline the process of collecting and consolidating large volumes of data, including over 15,000 records on internet services and tariffs, enabling further analysis and insights into trends and pricing during the specified period.

Why is it useful?
- Automation saves time and effort by automating the retrieval and consolidation of large datasets.

- Comprehensive Dataset: Collects over 15,000 records on internet services and tariffs in Peru from 2022 to 2025.

- Data Analysis: Enables analysis of trends, pricing patterns, and changes over the specified period.

- Error Reduction: Minimizes the risk of errors and inconsistencies in data collection.

- Reliable Insights: Provides accurate data for decision-making, research, and policy analysis.

I am currently in the process of data cleansing and analysis as part of a more extensive project.
